1. Who we are

WITSR Games is the brand under which an individual independent developer based in Quebec, Canada, creates and publishes the Android game WITSR — Why Is This So Ridiculous? (“the game”). In this policy, “we”, “us” and “our” mean WITSR Games.

Person in charge of the protection of personal information: the developer of WITSR Games, who can be reached at hellowitsr@gmail.com.

2. What this policy covers

This policy explains what information the game and this website (witsr.net) handle, why, and the choices you have. It applies to the current beta versions of the game distributed through Google Play.

Google Play itself, which you use to download the game, is operated by Google under its own terms and privacy policy.

3. Age

The game is intended for players aged 16 and older. It is not designed for children, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone under 16. If you are under 16, please do not use the game. If you believe someone under 16 has shared information with us, please contact us so that we can delete it.

4. Information that stays on your device

The game stores the following on your device. We do not receive it:

  • your game progress, such as levels completed, characters, upgrades and in-game rewards;
  • your settings, such as sound, graphics and your privacy choices;
  • any player name you enter in the game;
  • ratings or comments you give in the game's run-feedback prompt, which are currently kept on your device and not sent to us.

Uninstalling the game, or clearing its data in your device settings, deletes this information from your device. If you have turned on Android's backup to your Google account, Android may include the game's data in that backup, which Google stores in your account under its own terms. We cannot access your backups, and we cannot restore your progress for you.

5. Optional usage statistics and crash reports

The game includes two services from Google's Firebase platform, and you decide about each one separately. Each is switched off when the game is installed and stays off unless you switch it on (see section 6). You can switch on either one, both or neither. A service that is on sends the information listed for it below to Google, which provides these services to us (see section 7).

Usage statistics — Google Analytics for Firebase

In the game: USAGE STATISTICS — “Help us understand how players use WITSR.”

  • How the app is used: events that Firebase records automatically, such as the first time the app is opened, the start of each session, how long the app is in use, screens viewed and app updates. We do not currently add any custom game events.
  • Device and app information: for example device model, operating system version, language, time zone and the game's version.
  • Approximate location: country or region, which Google derives from your device's IP address.
  • Install source: how the app was installed, for example from Google Play.
  • Identifiers: random identifiers created for the game on your device, such as an app-instance ID and a Firebase installation ID, and other technical identifiers that Firebase includes in its reports. They do not contain your name, email address or phone number.

The game does not collect your name, email address, contacts, photos or precise location. We have turned off the collection of your device's Advertising ID, and Google signals (which links Analytics data to signed-in Google accounts) is turned off.

Crash reports — Firebase Crashlytics

In the game: CRASH REPORTS — “Help us identify and fix technical crashes.”

  • What went wrong: technical details of crashes and errors, such as error messages and the part of the game's code involved.
  • Device state at the time: for example device model, operating system version, available memory and storage, and screen orientation.
  • App and session information: the game's version, the time of the crash, basic session information that Firebase uses to calculate how often the game runs without crashing, and, only if usage statistics are also switched on, the recent usage events described above.
  • Identifiers: random identifiers such as a Crashlytics installation ID, a Firebase installation ID and a session ID. The list is not exhaustive; they do not contain your name, email address or phone number.

Why we use them

Usage statistics are used only to understand how players use WITSR. Crash reports are used only to identify and fix technical crashes. We rely on your consent for each one separately, and the game works the same whether or not you give it.

6. Your choices and how to change them

  • First launch: the game's main menu shows YOUR PRIVACY CHOICES, with a separate switch for USAGE STATISTICS and for CRASH REPORTS, both OFF, a SAVE MY CHOICES button and a link to this policy. Saving with both switches off declines both. If you close the notice without saving, neither usage statistics nor crash reports are sent, and the game asks again.
  • Changing your mind: Settings > Privacy has its own ON/OFF switch for each choice. Changing one never changes the other.
  • When you switch usage statistics off: they stop right away, and the analytics information stored on your device, including its app-instance ID, is reset.
  • When you switch crash reports off: because of how Firebase Crashlytics works, crash reporting only stops from the next time you open the game. Until then, Settings shows “STOPS AFTER RESTART”.
  • Crash information recorded while crash reports are off is deleted from your device instead of being sent, including if you switch crash reports on later.
  • Where your choices are kept: on your device. Resetting your game progress keeps them; uninstalling the game or clearing its data removes them, and the game will ask again.
  • Information already sent before you switched a choice off is not deleted right away. It is deleted when its retention period ends (see section 9), and you can ask us to delete it sooner, within the limits explained in section 11.

7. Who receives the information

When you switch them on, usage statistics and crash reports are sent to Google LLC, which provides Firebase and Google Analytics to us as a service provider under its data processing terms. All of our optional Google Analytics data-sharing settings are turned off, so Google uses the usage statistics only to provide and maintain the Analytics service for us. You can read more in the Firebase privacy information and the Google Privacy Policy.

We do not sell your information, we do not share it with advertisers, and the game has no advertising.

8. Where the information is processed

Google may process and store this information anywhere Google or its service providers have facilities, including outside Quebec and Canada. The laws of those places may differ from the laws where you live.

9. How long it is kept

The two services follow different rules:

  • Usage statistics (Google Analytics for Firebase): our retention setting is 14 months for device-level and event-level data. Later activity does not extend this period. Google deletes expired data automatically in monthly batches. This setting does not apply to summary reports that combine many players' data and do not single out a device; those can be kept longer.
  • Crash reports (Firebase Crashlytics): Google keeps crash details and the identifiers attached to them for 90 days, then starts deleting them from its live and backup systems. The 14-month Analytics setting does not apply to crash reports.
  • Other technical information that Firebase uses to run these services is kept under Google's own Firebase retention rules, described in the Firebase privacy information.

If you email us, we keep your message only as long as we need it to answer you and handle your request.

10. Security

Information sent to Firebase travels over encrypted connections. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, but we limit what is collected and who can access it.

11. Your rights and requests

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to access the personal information we hold about you, to have it corrected or deleted, and to withdraw your consent. To make a request, email hellowitsr@gmail.com.

What we can do depends on the information involved:

  • On your device: you can delete everything yourself at any time by uninstalling the game or clearing its data. In Settings > Privacy, switching usage statistics off withdraws that consent and resets the game's analytics identifier, and switching crash reports off withdraws that consent from the next time you open the game.
  • Information already sent to Google: the game does not ask who you are and does not show you the random identifiers that Firebase uses. Without one of those identifiers we usually cannot tell which records are yours, so we may not be able to find, copy or delete them individually. After you switch usage statistics off, the old analytics identifier is no longer stored on your device. In every case, this information is deleted automatically at the end of the periods in section 9.

If you write to us, we will tell you what we were able to do. We will not ask for more information than we need to answer your request.

You can also contact a privacy regulator, such as the Commission d'accès à l'information du Québec or the Office of the Privacy Commissioner of Canada, or the authority where you live.

12. This website

This website is hosted on GitHub Pages. GitHub may log technical information about visits, such as IP addresses, for security and operation, under the GitHub Privacy Statement. The website itself uses no cookies, no analytics and no advertising, and its font is served from this site.

13. Changes to this policy
