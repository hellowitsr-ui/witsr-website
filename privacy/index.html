1. Who we are and how we protect your information

WITSR Games is the name under which an independent developer based in Quebec, Canada, creates and publishes the Android game WITSR — Why Is This So Ridiculous? (“the game”). “We”, “us” and “our” mean WITSR Games.

Person in charge of the protection of personal information: the developer of WITSR Games, reachable at hellowitsr@gmail.com.

How we manage personal information. The person in charge approves this policy and our practices. We keep personal information only for the periods in section 13, then delete it. We respond to privacy requests within 30 days of receiving them, and check who is asking before we act (section 16). We record confidentiality incidents in a register and report those that present a risk of serious injury (section 15). We assess the privacy impact of new projects and of communications of information outside Quebec, and we use only service providers bound by written terms.

2. What this policy covers

This policy explains what information the game, our game servers and this website (witsr.net) handle, why, and the choices you have. It applies to the versions of the game distributed through Google Play. Google Play, Google Play Games and your Google account are operated by Google under Google's own terms and privacy policy.

3. Age

The game is intended for players aged 16 and older. It is not designed for children, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you are under 16, please do not use the game.

Parents and guardians: if you believe your child under 16 has used the game, contact us at hellowitsr@gmail.com. When we learn that a player is under 14, we delete their cloud account and its data promptly. You can also delete it right away on the child's phone in Settings > Delete Cloud Account.

4. Information stored on your phone

The game keeps on your phone: your game progress (levels, characters, classes, upgrades, Digits, Stars, XP and Gems); your settings, including your privacy and ad choices; a player name if you entered one in an early version; a short history of your recent runs and their rewards (up to 25 runs); up to three recovery copies of your save; and ratings you give in the optional run-feedback prompt, which are never sent to us.

Uninstalling the game or clearing its data deletes this information from your phone. If you use Android's backup to your Google account, Android may include the game's data in that backup, under Google's terms.

5. Your cloud account, cloud save and Gems

When an account is created. When the game is signed in to Google Play Games on your phone, it creates a WITSR cloud account automatically, using Google Firebase Authentication. We never ask for an email address or password. If you are not signed in to Play Games, no cloud account is created.

What we store (in our Google Firebase / Google Cloud project; the database and game server are in Montreal):

  • Account identifiers: a random WITSR account ID and your Google Play Games player ID. Firebase Authentication also keeps the Play Games display name and profile picture link that Google provides at sign-in. The game and our servers do not use them.
  • Your cloud save: a copy of your progress and settings, including the history of your recent runs and their rewards (up to 25 runs, which also shows which optional ads were watched in those runs), and a player name if you entered one.
  • Your Gem wallet: the Gems you earned (for example the Daily Login, challenges, the Weekly Bonus, milestones, Campaign Mastery, the tutorial reward), what you converted them into, and the records that prevent a reward or conversion from being applied twice. Our server is the authority for Gems.
  • A reserved purchase code: a one-way code derived from your account ID, reserved for purchases if they are ever offered.
  • Technical request logs: Google Cloud records technical details of requests to our servers, which can include your IP address. We keep only a small sample of these logs (about 1%), for security, abuse prevention and fixing problems.

How synchronization works. After a run, a claimed reward or another progress change, the game uploads your progress (grouping changes over a few seconds), and it checks the cloud copy when you return to the home screen or reopen the game. Uploads never happen during a run. If two phones changed your progress, the game asks you which progress to keep. Before a cloud copy replaces the progress on your phone, a recovery copy is kept on the phone.

Why: to save your progress and restore it on another phone, keep Gems correct, and prevent cheating and abuse.

6. Purchases

The game currently offers no in-app purchases, no subscriptions and no passes; the in-game Store shows “Coming soon”. Gems can only be earned by playing and have no cash value. Before anything is offered, we will update this policy. Payments would be processed by Google Play; we would never receive card details.

7. Advertising

The game shows ads from Google AdMob, a Google service:

  • Rewarded ads, which you choose to watch for an in-game reward (for example doubling a run's rewards, a Golden Leader, a revive or a Wheel respin, or extra Class Stars). The game is fully playable without them. A reward is given only when the ad reports that it was watched to the end.
  • Ads between Campaign levels (interstitials), shown occasionally after you continue from a Campaign result and never during play. You cannot skip the occasional interstitial in exchange for playing; you can still choose whether ads are personalized.

What Google collects when ads are requested or shown, according to Google: your device's IP address (which can reveal your approximate location), your advertising ID and app set ID, information about your device and the game, how you interact with ads, technical diagnostics, and your consent choices (described below). Google uses this to show and measure ads, limit how often you see the same ad, and prevent fraud, and Google is responsible for this processing under the Google Privacy Policy. Through Google's systems, ad requests can reach other advertising companies that bid to show ads. WITSR does not receive your advertising ID.

Personalized and non-personalized ads. Personalized ads use information about your interests. Non-personalized ads are not based on your past behaviour, but Google still uses some information, such as your IP address, device information and identifiers, for frequency capping, aggregated reporting, fraud prevention and to choose ads based on context.

  • European Economic Area, United Kingdom and Switzerland: Google's consent form asks for your choices before ads are requested. You can change them at any time in Settings > Ad Privacy.
  • US states with privacy laws: you can opt out of the sale or sharing of personal information for targeted advertising in Settings > Ad Privacy.
  • Everywhere else, including Canada and Quebec: ads are not personalized unless you turn on personalized ads in Settings > Ad Privacy. You can turn them off again at any time.
  • If Google's consent service cannot be reached and no earlier choice is known, the game requests no ads at all.

Your consent choices are stored on your phone and sent to Google with each ad request so that your choices are applied. You can also reset or delete your advertising ID in your Android settings (for example Settings > Google > Ads).

8. Optional usage statistics and crash reports

The game includes two Google Firebase services, and you decide about each one separately. Each is off when the game is installed and stays off unless you switch it on. The game works the same either way.

Usage statistics — Google Analytics for Firebase

In the game: USAGE STATISTICS — “Help us understand how players use WITSR and how its ads perform.” With it switched on, the game sends:

  • Automatic app usage recorded by Firebase: first open, sessions, time in the app, screens, app updates, and the ad impressions that Firebase records automatically for apps using AdMob.
  • Game events: Campaign levels and Infinity runs started and finished, with their outcome, duration, chosen difficulty, sections reached and rewards earned; the character and class used; branching-path, finale-difficulty and Wheel of Ridiculousness results; Special ability uses, revives and Golden Leaders used (and whether each was earned in play, won on the Wheel or received for watching an ad); tutorial progress.
  • Advertising events: which optional ads were offered, requested, shown, completed, skipped or failed to load; whether the reward was given; Google's estimate of the revenue from each ad shown; and whether test or real ads were used.
  • Device and app information: device model, operating system version, language, time zone, the game's version and build number.
  • Approximate location: country or region, derived by Google from your IP address.
  • Random identifiers: an app-instance ID and a Firebase installation ID.

Game and advertising events never include your name, your account ID, free text or anything you type. Firebase Analytics does not collect your advertising ID in WITSR, and Google signals is turned off.

If you switched usage statistics on before November 25, 2026 (test versions), our policy then said that only automatic app usage was collected. For you, the game keeps sending only that automatic app usage. The game and advertising events above are sent only if you switch usage statistics on again in a version released on or after that date.

Crash reports — Firebase Crashlytics

In the game: CRASH REPORTS — “Help us identify and fix technical crashes.” With it switched on, the game sends technical details of crashes and errors, the device's state at the time (model, operating system, available memory and storage), the game's version and build, session information and a random Crashlytics installation ID. If usage statistics is also on, a short list of the game events just before a crash is included.

Legal basis: your consent for each service, which you can withdraw at any time.

9. Rating and sharing WITSR

RATE WITSR in Settings opens the game's Google Play page. Ratings and reviews are handled by Google Play; we never ask for a particular rating and never reward rating. SHARE WITSR opens your phone's share menu with a short message you can edit; it goes only to the app and people you choose, and WITSR does not see what you share.

10. Your choices

  • Settings > Privacy: separate ON/OFF switches for usage statistics and crash reports. Turning usage statistics off stops Analytics at once and clears the Analytics data still stored on the phone. Turning crash reports off takes effect at the next launch.
  • Settings > Ad Privacy: Google's privacy options where they apply, or the personalized-ads choice elsewhere.
  • Settings > Delete Cloud Account: see section 14.
  • Google Play Games: if you don't use Play Games, no cloud account is created.

11. Who receives the information

  • Google LLC, as our service provider, runs Firebase (Authentication, Cloud Firestore, Cloud Functions, Analytics, Crashlytics) and Google Cloud under its data processing terms, and uses your account, cloud save and Gem records only to provide these services to us.
  • Google AdMob receives the advertising information in section 7 and uses it under Google's own terms and policies, as an independent controller.
  • Google Play processes Play Games sign-in under its own terms.
  • Google (Gmail) holds emails you send us.
  • GitHub hosts this website.

We do not sell your personal information, and we do not give your account, cloud save or Gem records to advertisers.

12. Where the information is processed

Our game server and the database holding cloud accounts, cloud saves and Gem records are in Google Cloud's Montreal region. Sign-in, technical logs, usage statistics, crash reports, advertising, Google Play and email are services that Google may operate in the United States and other countries; this website is hosted by GitHub in the United States. Google is a US company, and US authorities may be able to require it to provide information it holds, including information stored in Montreal.

As Quebec's privacy law requires, we have carried out a privacy impact assessment of these communications, considering the sensitivity of the information, the purposes, the protections in place (including Google's contractual terms, encryption and access limits) and the legal framework of the places concerned. The laws of those places may differ from the laws where you live.

13. How long it is kept

  • Cloud account, cloud save, Gem records and the Play Games name and picture link: as long as the account exists; we do not delete accounts for inactivity. Deleted from our database at once when you delete your account (section 14). Under the Google Cloud data processing terms that cover our Google Cloud project, Google then deletes any remaining copies of this data from its systems as soon as reasonably practicable and within a maximum of 180 days, unless the law requires it to keep them. This 180-day period applies only to the data in our Google Cloud project, not to the other Google services described in this policy, which follow the periods listed below.
  • Technical request logs: up to 30 days.
  • Usage statistics: 14 months for event-level and user-level data, then deleted automatically. Aggregated reports that do not identify a device can be kept longer.
  • Crash reports: 90 days.
  • Advertising data: kept by Google under its own policies.
  • Emails to us: deleted 12 months after your request is closed. A minimal record of each privacy request (date, type, account concerned, outcome; never the message itself) is kept for 3 years as evidence that we handled it.
  • Feedback form responses (including an optional contact email): deleted 12 months after they are received.
  • Beta testers group membership (your email address in the Google Group): kept while you are a member; removed when you leave the group, when you ask us, or when testing ends.

14. Deleting your account

In the game: go to Settings > Delete Cloud Account and confirm. Deletion happens right away (it needs an internet connection and Play Games sign-in). Cloud save then stays off on that phone until you turn it on again, which creates a new, empty account.

Without the game: follow the steps at witsr.net/delete-account or email hellowitsr@gmail.com. We respond within 30 days of receiving your request. If we can confirm that the account is yours (section 16), we delete it within that period and confirm; if we cannot, we tell you what we could not confirm.

What is deleted: your WITSR account and Play Games link (including the name and picture link), your cloud save, and your Gem wallet with its reward and conversion records. They are removed from our database immediately. Google Cloud, as our service provider, then deletes any remaining copies of this data from its systems within a maximum of 180 days, unless the law requires it to keep them.

What is not deleted by this:

  • the progress and recovery copies on your phone, until you uninstall the game or clear its data, and any Android backup in your Google account, which you control;
  • a technical marker containing only the deleted account ID, removed automatically within about two days, which prevents the account from being re-created by mistake;
  • technical request logs that may already contain your IP address, which expire within 30 days;
  • usage statistics and crash reports, which are linked to random identifiers rather than to your account and expire as described in section 13;
  • information held by Google as an independent provider: your Google account, Play Games profile and advertising data;
  • your emails to us and the minimal request record, as described in section 13.

15. Security and incidents

Information travels over encrypted connections. The game never accesses our database directly: every request goes through our server, which checks the signed-in account first, and the database refuses all other access. We limit what is collected and who can access it. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure. If a confidentiality incident presents a risk of serious injury, we will notify the people concerned and the Commission d'accès à l'information du Québec, as the law requires, and record it in our incident register.

16. Your rights and requests

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to access the personal information we hold about you, have it corrected or deleted, withdraw your consent, receive a copy in a structured, commonly used format (or have it sent to someone you designate), and be informed about technologies that can identify, locate or profile you (in WITSR: personalized advertising, off by default in Quebec, and the approximate location in optional usage statistics).

How to ask: email hellowitsr@gmail.com. We respond within 30 days of receiving your request; checking that the account is yours happens within that period.

How we check it is you. WITSR does not store email addresses, so we must confirm that the account belongs to you before acting:

  • The simplest way to delete your account is in the game (section 14), which proves it through your Play Games sign-in.
  • Otherwise, please tell us your Google Play Games name, roughly when you started and last played, and some details only the player would know, such as your highest Campaign level, main character, approximate Gem balance or Class level. We act only if the details match one account, and we confirm with you by email before deleting anything or sending you a copy.
  • Never send us passwords, identity documents or payment information. If you send them anyway, we delete them.
  • If we cannot establish with sufficient confidence that the account is yours, we will not disclose any personal information by email. We will tell you what we could not confirm.

Usage statistics and crash reports already sent are linked only to random identifiers that the game does not show you, so we usually cannot tell which records are yours. They are deleted automatically at the end of the periods in section 13. For information held by Google, use Settings > Ad Privacy, your Android ad settings or your Google account's tools.

You can also contact a privacy regulator, such as the Commission d'accès à l'information du Québec, the Office of the Privacy Commissioner of Canada or the authority where you live.

17. This website

This website is hosted on GitHub Pages. GitHub may log technical information about visits, such as IP addresses, under the GitHub Privacy Statement. The website itself collects no personal information: no accounts, no cookies, no analytics, no advertising.

The website links to our feedback form on Google Forms and to the WITSR Beta Testers group on Google Groups, operated by Google under the Google Privacy Policy. As their owner, WITSR Games can see the email addresses of group members and the responses to the form, and uses them only to run testing and follow up on feedback. Giving a contact email in the feedback form is optional.

Where the law requires your consent for a new or expanded use, we will ask for it again, for example in the game, before that processing begins.
