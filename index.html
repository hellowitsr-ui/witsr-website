Meet the leaders

Three playable characters, each with their own weapon and special ability. None of them asked for this.

Clarissa

Berry Gun · Special: Berry Bad Day

Clarissa was once a perfectly normal rabbit until a robot stole the last carrot at her local grocery store. She filed a complaint. The robots responded by declaring her a threat to national security. Now armed with a berry gun and an army of questionable volunteers, she's waging war against every robot she sees. All she wanted was a single carrot.

Teresa

Bottle Caps · Special: Tidal Wave

Teresa was supposed to be a premium insulated water bottle. Instead, a factory robot assembled her backwards, leaving her unable to keep water cold, hot, or even inside. After receiving a one-star review from her own manufacturer, she snapped. Now she launches bottle caps at every robot she encounters. Dishwasher safe? Absolutely not.

Bob

Ninja Stars · Special: Meditation

Bob was once the prized pupil of the Rat Who Shall Not Be Named. Unfortunately, his family disowned him after a robot defeated him in a martial arts tournament. Humiliated and disgraced, Bob now fights to restore his family's honor and prove he's a true ninja. Unfortunately, the robot that defeated him was a vending machine.
