Join the Closed Beta

WITSR is currently available for Android closed beta testing on Google Play. It takes four quick steps and a few minutes. All you need is an Android phone and the Google account you use for the Play Store.

Show me how Submit Feedback

Four steps to play

Do them in order, on your Android phone if you can. Use the same Google account for every step: the one your phone's Play Store is signed in with.

  1. 1

    Join our Google Group

    Join the WITSR Beta Testers group with the Google account that is signed in to the Play Store on your Android phone. This is how Google Play knows you're invited.

    Joining the group is only the invitation. It does not make you a tester yet, so keep going to step 2.

    Join the Google Group (opens in a new tab)
  2. 2

    Become a Google Play tester

    Open the Google Play testing page and tap Become a tester. This is the step that officially opts you in to the beta. If the button doesn't appear, check that you're signed in with the same account you used for the group, and that you joined the group first.

    Become a tester (opens in a new tab)
  3. 3

    Install WITSR

    Open WITSR on Google Play and install it, using the same Google account. After you opt in, it can take a little while (sometimes a few hours) before the game is available to you. If Google Play says the app isn't available or can't be found, check that you finished step 2 with the same account, then wait a bit and try again. The game only shows up for people who have opted in.

    Get WITSR on Google Play (opens in a new tab)
  4. 4

    Submit Feedback

    Play, then tell us what you think. Bugs, crashes, gameplay issues, ideas: everything helps. You can come back to this page and send feedback as often as you like.

    Submit Feedback (opens in a new tab)

Please stay for 14 days

Google Play requires new games to be tested by a group of testers for at least 14 days in a row before they can be released. Please stay opted in for at least 14 consecutive days after you become a tester. You don't have to play every day, but leaving the test early can reset our count. Thank you, it really helps!

Good to know

Android only

The beta is for Android phones through Google Play. There is no iPhone version at the moment.

Ages 16+

WITSR is intended for players aged 16 and older.

Stuck?

Most problems come from using two different Google accounts. Make sure the group, the tester page and the Play Store all use the same one. Still stuck? Write to hellowitsr@gmail.com.

Your information

This website collects nothing. The Google Group, Google Play and the feedback form are Google services, handled under the Google Privacy Policy (opens in a new tab). As owners of the group and the form, we can see the email address you join the group with and the answers you submit. See our privacy policy for the game.
