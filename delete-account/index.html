Delete your WITSR account

WITSR (Why Is This So Ridiculous?, by WITSR Games) creates a cloud account when you play signed in to Google Play Games, so your progress can be saved online and restored on another phone. You can delete that account and its data at any time, in the game or by request.

Two ways to delete

  1. 1

    In the game (fastest)

    Open WITSR, go to Settings, tap Delete Cloud Account and confirm. The deletion happens right away. You need an internet connection and to be signed in to Google Play Games on that phone.

    Progress saved on that phone stays on the phone. Cloud save stays off there until you turn it back on, which creates a new, empty cloud account.

  2. 2

    By email, without the game

    If you no longer have WITSR installed, email us from any address. WITSR doesn't store email addresses, so we must check that the account is yours. Please include:

    • your Google Play Games name (as shown in the Play Games app),
    • roughly when you started and last played,
    • two or more game details only the player would know, such as your highest Campaign level, main character, approximate Gem balance or Class level.

    We respond within 30 days of receiving your request. We act only if these details match a single account, and we reply to confirm with you before deleting anything; we then delete the account within that period and confirm. If we cannot confirm that the account is yours, we tell you what we could not confirm and we never send personal information by email. Never send passwords, identity documents or payment information.

    Email a deletion request

    Or write to hellowitsr@gmail.com with the subject "WITSR account deletion request".

What is deleted, what is kept

Deleted

  • Your WITSR cloud account (sign-in record, account ID and Play Games link, including the Play Games display name and picture link).
  • Your cloud save (game progress, player name, settings stored in it).
  • Your Gem wallet and its records (rewards claimed, conversions).

They are removed from our database immediately. Google Cloud, as our service provider, then deletes any remaining copies of this data from its systems within a maximum of 180 days, unless the law requires it to keep them. This period applies to the data in our Google Cloud project; other Google services follow the periods listed below.

Kept

  • There are no in-app purchases today. If purchases are introduced in the future, their records will be kept as the law requires.
  • A short-lived technical marker of the deleted account ID, removed automatically within about two days. It stops the account from being re-created by accident.
  • Progress and recovery copies saved on your phone, until you uninstall the game or clear its data, and any Android backup in your Google account, which you control.
  • Technical request logs that may contain your IP address, which expire within 30 days.
  • Optional usage statistics and crash reports, which are linked to random identifiers rather than your account and expire after 14 months and 90 days.
  • Your emails to us (deleted 12 months after the request is closed) and a minimal record of the request (kept 3 years).
  • Feedback form responses (deleted 12 months after they are received) and beta testers group membership (removed when you leave the group, ask us, or testing ends), which are not part of your cloud account.

Google services

Your Google Play Games profile belongs to your Google account; manage it in the Play Games app. Ads, crash reports and usage statistics are processed by Google under its own policies; see our privacy policy. You can reset or delete your advertising ID in your Android settings (for example Settings > Google > Ads).

Questions

Write to hellowitsr@gmail.com. Parents or guardians of a player under 16 can also ask us to delete the child's account. See also our privacy policy.
